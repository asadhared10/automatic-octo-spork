Our Heritage

Crafting Unrivaled Culinary Artistry Since 2011

"Cooking is an act of passion, storytelling, and reverence for nature's finest ingredients."

Founded by Executive Chef Antoine Laurent, Savor & Vine was born out of a profound passion for bringing farm-to-table freshness into an exquisite fine dining setting. Every dish tells a story of sustainable farming, centuries-old techniques, and innovative culinary vision.

Nestled in the heart of the culinary district, our intimate dining room offers an immersive atmosphere where candlelit tables, sommelier pairings, and attentive service turn every dinner into a cherished memory.
